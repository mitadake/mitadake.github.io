Featured Research

GGUF k-quant Content-Injection Attack

Researched and built a novel quantization attack for LLMs: models stay benign under full-precision (FP) inference but become malicious after GGUF k-quantization. Demonstrated that safety at FP does not guarantee safety post-quantization.

Key Results:

  • Q4_K_M: ASR up to 82-83% (FP ASR: 0-3%)
  • Other quants (Q2-Q6): 38-68% quantized ASR with 2-18% FP ASR
  • Models: Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct, Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct
LLM Security Quantization GGUF Adversarial ML PyTorch LoRA
